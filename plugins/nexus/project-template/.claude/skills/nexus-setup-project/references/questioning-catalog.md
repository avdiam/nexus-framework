### Five Techniques

| Technique | When to Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Challenge vagueness** | Abstract terms without concrete meaning | "When you say 'better performance', what specific metric are you targeting?" |
| **Make abstract concrete** | Concept clear but not grounded | "Can you give me an example of what {X} would look like in practice?" |
| **Surface assumptions** | Answer implies unstated beliefs | "You mentioned {X} — is it a hard constraint or a preference?" |
| **Find edges** | Need boundary understanding | "What would be just outside the boundary of {X}?" |
| **Reveal motivation** | 'What' is clear but 'why' is missing | "Why does {X} matter? What problem does it solve?" |

### Dimension Checklist

| Dimension | Key Questions |
|---|---|
| **Vision** | What are we creating? Why does it matter? How will we know it succeeded? |
| **Users** | Who benefits? How do they interact? What do they need to accomplish? |
| **Problem** | What pain or gap exists today? What causes it? What happens if unsolved? |
| **Scope** | What's included? What's explicitly excluded? Where are the boundaries? |
| **Constraints** | What limits exist (time, resources, technology)? What's non-negotiable? |
| **Prior art** | What exists already? What can we learn from? What should we avoid repeating? |

### Application by Wizard Depth

| Depth | Approach |
|---|---|
| Light | Accept after one gentle probe. Techniques only for critical gaps. |
| Standard | Push once per gap with relevant technique. Accept after one round. |
| Thorough | Push 2-3 times per gap. Multiple techniques. Socratic depth. |
