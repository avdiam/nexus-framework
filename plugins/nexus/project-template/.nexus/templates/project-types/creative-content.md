[Section: Risk-Catalog]
- risk: "Scope creep through endless revision cycles"
  probability: "High"
  impact: "Medium"
  mitigation_hint: "Define revision budget upfront (e.g., 2 major rounds), tie revisions to specific criteria not subjective preference"
- risk: "Subjective quality standards — 'good enough' means different things to different people"
  probability: "High"
  impact: "Medium"
  mitigation_hint: "Establish quality rubric during creative direction phase, reference concrete examples of target quality"
- risk: "Inconsistency across content pieces — voice, style, or quality varies"
  probability: "Medium"
  impact: "Medium"
  mitigation_hint: "Create and enforce style guide, conduct consistency audits, use templates for recurring formats"
- risk: "Audience mismatch — content doesn't resonate with intended audience"
  probability: "Medium"
  impact: "High"
  mitigation_hint: "Define audience clearly during vision phase, test early pieces with representative audience, adapt based on feedback"
- risk: "Creative burnout or block during production phase"
  probability: "Medium"
  impact: "Medium"
  mitigation_hint: "Build buffer time into schedule, allow flexibility in production order, maintain inspiration sources"
- risk: "Rights and permissions issues with referenced or included material"
  probability: "Low"
  impact: "High"
  mitigation_hint: "Audit rights requirements early, establish clear permissions workflow, use original content where possible"
[/Section: Risk-Catalog]
