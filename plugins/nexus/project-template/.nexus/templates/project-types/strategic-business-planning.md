[Section: Deliverable-Templates]
### MVP Examples
- name: "Situation Analysis"
  description: "Evidence-based assessment of the current state: market position, capabilities, challenges, opportunities"
  quality_criteria: "Claims supported by data, stakeholders recognize the picture as accurate, gaps acknowledged"
- name: "Strategic Recommendation"
  description: "Prioritized set of recommendations with rationale, trade-offs, and implementation implications"
  quality_criteria: "Options compared fairly, trade-offs explicit, recommendation clearly justified"
- name: "Roadmap / Action Plan"
  description: "Sequenced plan for executing the strategy with milestones, owners, and dependencies"
  quality_criteria: "Actions are specific enough to execute, sequencing reflects dependencies, progress measurable"

### Enhanced Examples
- name: "Scenario Analysis"
  description: "Multiple future scenarios with strategic implications and contingency plans"
  quality_criteria: "Scenarios are plausible and distinct, each has a clear strategic response"
- name: "Business Case"
  description: "Financial and strategic justification for the recommended approach"
  quality_criteria: "Assumptions explicit, ROI calculated with sensitivity analysis, risks quantified"
- name: "Stakeholder Alignment Package"
  description: "Presentations, one-pagers, and briefing materials tailored to different audiences"
  quality_criteria: "Key message consistent across formats, adapted to each audience's concerns"

### Future Examples
- name: "Strategic Review Framework"
  description: "Recurring review process to assess strategy execution and adapt to changing conditions"
  quality_criteria: "Review cadence defined, leading indicators tracked, adaptation triggers specified"
- name: "Capability Development Plan"
  description: "Plan for building organizational capabilities needed to execute the strategy"
  quality_criteria: "Gaps identified, development paths defined, timeline realistic"

### Categorization Guidance
"MVP = the strategic question is answered with a defensible recommendation. Enhanced = the recommendation is quantified, scenario-tested, and stakeholder-ready. Future = the organization can adapt the strategy as conditions change."
[/Section: Deliverable-Templates]

[Section: Phase-Templates]
### Simple (2-3 phases)
- phase: "Research & Analysis"
  objective: "Gather evidence, analyze the situation, identify options"
  typical_deliverables: "Situation analysis, option inventory, initial assessment"
  milestone: "Strategic landscape understood with clear options identified"
- phase: "Recommendation & Planning"
  objective: "Evaluate options, formulate recommendation, build action plan"
  typical_deliverables: "Strategic recommendation, roadmap, stakeholder briefing"
  milestone: "Recommendation delivered and stakeholders aligned"

### Standard (3-4 phases)
- phase: "Situation Assessment"
  objective: "Understand the landscape: market, competition, internal capabilities, stakeholder needs"
  typical_deliverables: "Situation analysis, stakeholder input summary, data collection"
  milestone: "Current state documented with stakeholder agreement on the picture"
- phase: "Option Development"
  objective: "Generate strategic options, analyze trade-offs, model scenarios"
  typical_deliverables: "Option analysis, scenario models, trade-off framework"
  milestone: "Viable options defined with clear trade-offs articulated"
- phase: "Recommendation & Alignment"
  objective: "Select preferred strategy, build consensus, develop implementation plan"
  typical_deliverables: "Strategic recommendation, business case, stakeholder alignment"
  milestone: "Recommendation approved by decision-makers"
- phase: "Planning & Handoff"
  objective: "Translate strategy into actionable roadmap, define metrics, hand off to execution"
  typical_deliverables: "Implementation roadmap, success metrics, governance framework"
  milestone: "Execution team has clear plan with measurable milestones"

### Complex (5-6 phases)
- phase: "Framing & Scoping"
  objective: "Define the strategic question precisely, identify stakeholders, plan the analysis"
  typical_deliverables: "Strategic brief, stakeholder map, analysis plan"
  milestone: "Strategic question agreed with clear boundaries and success criteria"
- phase: "Evidence Gathering"
  objective: "Collect data from market, customers, competitors, internal operations"
  typical_deliverables: "Market research, competitive analysis, internal capability assessment"
  milestone: "Evidence base sufficient to support strategic analysis"
- phase: "Deep Analysis"
  objective: "Analyze evidence, identify patterns, model scenarios, stress-test assumptions"
  typical_deliverables: "Analytical findings, scenario models, assumption registry"
  milestone: "Key insights validated with evidence, assumptions tested"
- phase: "Strategy Formulation"
  objective: "Develop strategic options, evaluate against criteria, select preferred approach"
  typical_deliverables: "Strategic options, evaluation framework, preliminary recommendation"
  milestone: "Preferred strategy selected with documented rationale"
- phase: "Stakeholder Alignment"
  objective: "Present findings, build consensus, incorporate feedback, finalize recommendation"
  typical_deliverables: "Stakeholder presentations, feedback synthesis, final recommendation"
  milestone: "Decision-makers aligned on strategy"
- phase: "Implementation Planning"
  objective: "Translate strategy into executable roadmap with governance and measurement"
  typical_deliverables: "Implementation roadmap, governance framework, KPIs, review cadence"
  milestone: "Execution plan approved and ownership assigned"

### Phase Naming Guidance
"Use strategic planning language: Assessment, Analysis, Formulation, Alignment, Planning. Avoid technical terms (Pipeline, Deployment) and academic terms (Literature Review, Methodology)."
[/Section: Phase-Templates]

[Section: Risk-Catalog]
- risk: "Changing requirements — strategic context shifts during the project"
  probability: "Medium"
  impact: "High"
  mitigation_hint: "Build adaptability into the plan, use scenario analysis, define trigger points for strategy revision"
- risk: "Stakeholder disagreement on strategic direction"
  probability: "High"
  impact: "High"
  mitigation_hint: "Align on evaluation criteria before presenting options, involve key stakeholders throughout not just at the end"
- risk: "Evidence gaps — insufficient data to support confident recommendations"
  probability: "Medium"
  impact: "Medium"
  mitigation_hint: "Identify data needs early, use multiple evidence sources, acknowledge gaps explicitly in recommendations"
- risk: "Analysis paralysis — continuous analysis without converging on a recommendation"
  probability: "Medium"
  impact: "Medium"
  mitigation_hint: "Set decision deadlines, define 'good enough' evidence thresholds, separate analysis from decision"
- risk: "Plan without execution — strategy approved but never implemented"
  probability: "Medium"
  impact: "High"
  mitigation_hint: "Include implementation planning in scope, assign ownership and milestones, define review cadence"
- risk: "Anchoring to existing strategy — not genuinely exploring alternatives"
  probability: "Medium"
  impact: "Medium"
  mitigation_hint: "Include a 'clean sheet' option in analysis, challenge assumptions about current approach, seek outside perspectives"
[/Section: Risk-Catalog]
